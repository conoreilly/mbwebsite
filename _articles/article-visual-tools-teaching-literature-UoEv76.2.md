Thanks to The English Association for providing a forum to share practice and pedagogy across traditional subject boundaries.

In this article published in The use of English journal, I examine one way of using digital technology in the Classics classroom to enhance the teaching of literature. Through platforms such as canva, emerging visual tools allow us to create precise visual images of textual pictures with implications for the understanding and retention.

You can read the full article [here](https://englishassociation.ac.uk/the-use-of-english-76-2-spring-2025/).
